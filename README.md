# The World in One Picture — Friday 20 November 2026

A single 1024×1024 image summing up the world's news of Friday 20 November
2026, for the make.fun bounty
[news-image-20261120](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120).
`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the image held in this repo
to `/output/entry.png`. It runs offline and is byte-identical on every run.

## How to run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
# out/entry.png — 1024×1024 PNG, 131,115 bytes
```

`render.py` is the script that drew `entry.png` (Python + Pillow, original
illustration only — no photos, no logos, no network). Re-running it
reproduces the same image.

## What the image shows

All facts were checked against reputable reporting on Fri 20 Nov 2026
(~01:10 UTC / 07:10 Dhaka). The game launched on Thu 19 Nov; the image
covers its first full day, as reported on 20 Nov.

1. **GTA VI takes over the world** — a day after launch: 99/100 from
   critics, record livestreams (132m hours watched in one day) and about
   $1.8bn in sales. (CNN, 20 Nov 2026)
2. **Three leaders, one table** — Trump, Xi and Putin hold rare three-way
   talks in Shenzhen on nuclear risks and issue a joint "peace declaration",
   but with no breakthrough on Ukraine. (AP / Manila Times, 20 Nov 2026)
3. **Crimea Bridge hit again** — Ukraine strikes the bridge for a third
   time this week; Putin vows to hit back hard and drops any energy truce.
   (Japan Times / The Standard, 20 Nov 2026)
4. **Tankers burn near Hormuz** — three oil tankers catch fire after hitting
   mines east of the Strait of Hormuz as Iran steps up attacks, while oil
   prices slide. (Japan Times / NDTV, 20 Nov 2026)
5. **Myanmar guilty of genocide** — the top UN court finds Myanmar committed
   state-sponsored genocide against the Muslim Rohingya.
   (Japan Times / Manila Times, 19–20 Nov 2026)
6. **AI IPO soars, stocks slump** — Anthropic debuts at $83 a share, briefly
   worth $2 trillion, yet Wall Street falls 2% to seven-month lows.
   (Reuters / The Standard, 20 Nov 2026)
7. **Cyclone Akvan hits India** — the severe storm makes landfall on India's
   east coast; 120,000 people evacuated, dozens of trains cancelled.
   (NDTV / Mathrubhumi, 20 Nov 2026)
8. **Also today** — Henry Nicholls hits 113* as New Zealand reach 243/5 vs
   India in Wellington; NASA's Voyager 1 phones home from a light-day away;
   Riyadh's tallest tower is damaged in a Houthi strike.
